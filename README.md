# Drawing World

Draw a creature on paper, snap a photo, and it drops into a little island world as a paper cutout, then puffs up into a 3D body, waddles around, and chirps and says hi when you tap it. Every drawing adds another resident, and the island is saved on your device.

**Play:** https://zcloud8891.github.io/drawing-world/

- 📷 **Snap a drawing** opens the phone camera (or a photo picker / file picker).
- ✏️ **Try a sample** uses built-in sample crayon drawings (generated in code, no real photos).
- Tap a creature to hear it. Drag to look around, pinch to zoom. 🧹 starts a fresh island.

Everything runs in the browser: the cutout, the creature's name and voice, and the 3D body. Photos never leave the device and nothing is uploaded. Residents are saved in this browser's localStorage.

Three.js r160 (MIT) is vendored in `vendor/`.
